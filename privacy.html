Privacy Policy

Lewis Dutel Daily Brief · Effective September 19, 2026

Who this covers

Lewis Dutel operates the Lewis Dutel Daily Brief, an automated SMS notification service registered under the brand name Lewis Dutel ME Account. Messages are sent to the account owner's own mobile number. There are no other subscribers, and no third party can enroll.

What is collected

The program holds one mobile phone number, which belongs to the account owner, and the delivery records the messaging provider generates for each message sent to it. Message content consists of counts of outstanding items drawn from the account owner's own mail, messages and notebooks. The full summary is sent separately by email.

How it is used

The mobile number is used only to deliver these notifications. Delivery records are used only to confirm that a message was sent and to diagnose a failure to deliver.

Sharing

No information collected by this program is sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for marketing. Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. The only party that handles the data besides the account owner is Twilio, the messaging provider that transmits each message to the carrier.

Retention

Delivery records are retained by the messaging provider under its own retention policy. No separate database of recipients is kept.
