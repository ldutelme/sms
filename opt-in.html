SMS Notifications

Lewis Dutel Daily Brief

This is where the account holder enrolls in SMS notifications for the Lewis Dutel Daily Brief, sent under the brand name Lewis Dutel ME Account. Enrollment is optional. The account functions normally without it.

Mobile number

What you receive

A short message reporting how many items are outstanding in the account holder's workflow tracking system. The full summary is sent separately by email. After enrolling, you receive one confirmation text. Nothing is sent to any other person, and no third party can enroll a number here.

Frequency

Up to 2 messages per day.

Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any message to end them at any time. After replying STOP you will receive one confirmation message and nothing further.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, or email lewis@mydutel.com.

Your information

Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
