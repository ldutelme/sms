Program name

Lewis Dutel Daily Brief

Description

An automated notification service operated by Lewis Dutel under the brand name Lewis Dutel ME Account. Up to twice each day it sends a short text message to the account owner's own mobile number, reporting how many items are outstanding across the account owner's mail, messages and notebooks. The full summary is sent separately by email. The account owner is the only recipient.

Message frequency

Up to 2 messages per day.

Message and data rates may apply.

How to stop messages

Reply STOP to any message to end them at any time. After replying STOP you will receive one confirmation message and nothing further.
